# space — atlas, with the level above grown out of it

Atlas's instrument, whole — every facet, clock, rail, graph and census —
on a foundation where NOTHING about the session is declared: the ladder is
computed by chirality, ambiguity plugs its resolver only when the engine
refuses, transpilation is a licensed cast, and the session's visual form
is THE STRATA — readings at their levels, drawn as their own miniatures.

## What changed from atlas (everything else is atlas, feature-for-feature)

- **The ladder is COMPUTED.** `FIXTURE_FILES` names files, nothing more.
  A text climbs iff some flavour's metagrammar accepts it (tried against
  the engine, cached — `grammar_flavour_of`); the climb tops out at the
  spelling fixpoint; the export rung exists iff the flavour can export.
  The per-fixture if/elif ladders are gone from both `Subject` and
  `Session`.
- **The resolver plugs itself.** No fixture declares `first`: the bare
  parse is tried, and only the engine's own ambiguity refusal reaches
  for the plug. Even `corrupt_at` derives from chirality.
- **The strip is dead.** The masthead carries one chip — where you are.
  Clicking it (the ∴) pulls back to THE STRATA: a full-screen layer,
  rows by stratum (fixpoint above, grammar readings, documents, the
  outward/instrument row below), each reading a card with its live span
  -density band, counts, seconds, faithfulness, and its plug if one is
  in. Unvisited rungs are dim until traveled. Esc returns. `?strata`
  pins it for screenshots.
- **Transpilation is a ghost card.** Beside stratum 1: `≅ respell
  through <flavour>` — licence tried against the emitter and quoted
  (json.gbnf: refused by ABNF and EBNF over `IrNot`, words shown;
  amb/decide: licensed both ways). Taking a licensed ghost respells the
  base grammar, climbs it under ITS metagrammar, and travels there —
  `POST /cast`, census-gated with the peer's round-trip.
- Wire additions: `GET /strata`, `POST /cast transpile <flavour>`.

## The chain (the menu)

One column per THING in reading order — a thing IS its stratum, so that is
the column's header tag, never a second axis; the instrument stands beside
the chain, not in it. The menu carries READINGS only: click one to travel.
Deep-link with `?strata`.

## The rooms (in the instrument)

Values, the compiler and the artefacts are rooms, not menu entries. They
open in the `rooms` FACET — a real facet in the arrange tree, so it drags
on the same seams, tabs, docks and pops as the reader or the spine.
`?rooms` opens its index; `?place=<id>` opens one directly.

## The places — rooms typed by what the subject IS

The parsing window is for READINGS only. Everything else opens as its own
kind of room, reached from the strata grid (rows = levels, columns =
things) and deep-linkable with `?place=<id>`:

Every place room is a real facet layout: regions with headers and hairline
seams you can drag.

- **value** — three facets: the REDUCTION plug and its candidates (the
  model fold → a model · the flavour's reducer → an IR value · the flavour
  object itself — different codomains, not different buttons); the value
  drawn as IR (identity: one object reached N times is ONE node with N
  edges; tier; absence; the IrLambda refusal), zoomable child by child;
  and the operations on it (compile it · respell through another flavour,
  each licence tried and quoted).
- **compiler** — ONE room, four facets: the pipeline with its hoisting
  delta, canonical and codegen each with their rule graph (flat · arcs ·
  3d) and spelling, and the binding view.
- **artefacts** — the family compressed to a selector (ir value · twin ·
  model value · plain value — species by the reduction's codomain), each
  row carrying its load-back witnesses, then the selected file.

Plugs are stated on the reading card (reduction · resolver · vocabulary);
transpilation is an operation on a value, never a place in the strata.
