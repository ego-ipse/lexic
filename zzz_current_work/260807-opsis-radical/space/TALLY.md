- **Places are real facets now.** `place` joins `FACETS` with the others,

  the reader; `‹ back` walks the stack; `/place?id=index` gives the facet

  cards in the menu — they are rooms, reached under ROOMS in the
  instrument. The menu went from four bands of cards back to what worked.
- **Column order fixed.** It had been sorted by the minimum level in a
  lane, which let OUTWARD rungs (level −3) drag the metagrammar to the
  front and put the instrument at "stratum 0". Chain order now ignores
  non-chain rungs, the instrument sorts last and is labelled as itself,
  and strata are numbered by CHAIN RANK (document 0 → grammar 1 →
  metagrammar 2), not by a raw internal level.
- **The IR representation rebuilt.** It was a flat list of a node's
  children — a table of contents, not a value. It is now the DAG drawn as
