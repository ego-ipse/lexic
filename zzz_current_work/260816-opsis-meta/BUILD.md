## The render-measurement law (user ruling, 2026-08-16)

Earlier attempts failed because the implementer ASSUMED what the page
rendered instead of measuring it. Binding for every visual stage:

- No claim about a rendered state without a MEASUREMENT of that state:
  the browser probe drives the real handlers and writes verdicts
  (`?probe=1` → document.title), geometry is asserted from the rendered
  boxes (hit-test the PAINTED boxes, never recompute), and a screenshot
  is only evidence once OPENED AND LOOKED AT — a census is not a
  screenshot and a screenshot is not a look.
- The probe runs at 1× and 2×, checks what only a browser can (canvas
  sized to its box, planes on their sent geometry, a character the
  width the frame believes, controls that are real controls), and every
  facet lands a resize fact (track BOTH bitmap dimensions).
- "Looks like the thing it replaces" is settled by opening space_3 and
  the mock beside the build — photograph first, compare, then claim.
- A visual defect report from the user outranks any green census: drive
  the instrument, reproduce, then fix.
