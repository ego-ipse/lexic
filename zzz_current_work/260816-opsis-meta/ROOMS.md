from WAYFORWARD: a room with nothing to do is a gate failure; every facet
here names its gestures or does not exist.

## Cross-room laws (the gate's standing section)

1. One cursor; every facet showing the subject washes it in the frame it
   changed.
2. Every edit is a re-reading; a refusal keeps the typed text and the
   measured frontier.
3. PENDING/STALE/ABSENT are drawn states with words, never blanks.
4. Every count that is bounded says what it dropped.
5. Every door leads somewhere and BACK returns; a deferral is an absent
   door or a drawn refusal, never a broken door.
6. Element blocks on the wire agree with the pixels (the gate counts
   them per frame — INTERFACES §1).

## The mass law (added 2026-08-16, user correction)

7. **A facet shows the subject's TRUE MASS.** The parsing room earns its
   keep at 12,219 spans; every other room owes the same density — the
   family with its real numbers, a payload's literals as mass strips, a
   twin's whole roster, a census's full counts. Under pressure a facet
   derives less (bands, folds, "N more"), never clips — but a room that
   draws five chips for a rich subject is a register failure, and a mock
   below the subject's mass is a false reference that breeds sparse
   rooms. Mock acceptance includes mass.
